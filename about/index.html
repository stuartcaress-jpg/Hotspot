About ZELVUN

Meet. Share. Belong.

ZELVUN is built to help people discover groups, clubs, hobbies and events around shared interests — from foraging, D&D and chess to running, language exchange, music, dancing, cinema and outdoor activities.

A simpler way to find your people

Search for an interest, explore groups and events, and use local discovery to look for clubs and communities in an area you choose. The beta does not require an account or precise location.

Built with community in mind

ZELVUN is starting with a curated beta. More account-based community features, organiser tools and shared group participation can be added as the platform grows.
